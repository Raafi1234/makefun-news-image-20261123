## 1. Huang returns as Nvidia chief (lead story)

- Nvidia's board voted on Monday 23 November 2026 to reinstate Jensen
  Huang as chief executive, less than two weeks after he resigned on
  12 November amid reports of affairs and an alleged relationship with a
  woman the US government is investigating as a suspected Chinese spy.
  President Donald Trump intervened directly, telling CNBC he spoke to
  the board and "made the deal"; Vice-President JD Vance also pressed the
  board over the weekend. The board said it was "satisfied with respect
  to Mr. Huang's candor and ability to lead NVIDIA" and that it had
  received input from "the senior levels of the United States
  government" that the return posed no national security risk.
- Nvidia shares closed up 31.5% at $165.05, adding nearly $1 trillion in
  market value and making Nvidia again the world's second-largest company
  by market value, behind Apple. The Nasdaq rose 6.5% and the Dow more
  than 1,400 points.
- The US Commerce Department added Singapore-based Megaspeed, the cloud
  firm at the centre of the espionage investigation, to its trade
  blacklist (entity list) on Monday, barring it from buying US-made
  semiconductors without a licence.
- Sources: Reuters (via The Daily Star / TBS News, 23 Nov 2026); BBC
  News, 23 Nov 2026; Associated Press (via WMAL, 23 Nov 2026); New York
  Times, 23 Nov 2026.

## 2. NATO in emergency session after missiles land in Poland

- NATO's North Atlantic Council met in emergency session in Brussels on
  Monday 23 November 2026 after Poland confirmed that three Russian
  Kh-101 cruise missiles had landed on Polish territory on Friday
  20 November. NATO announced it would send early-warning (AWACS)
  aircraft to Poland.
- Sources: BBC News, 23 Nov 2026; Reuters (via Bermuda Broadcasting /
  BusinessWorld), 23 Nov 2026.

## 3. US drone helped catch "El Chapo Isidro"

- Continuation, stated explicitly: the capture itself happened very
  late on Friday 20 November, when Mexican special forces raided
  Guasave, Sinaloa, and captured Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as
  "El Chapo Isidro", one of the FBI's Ten Most Wanted fugitives, with
  at least 21 people killed in the fighting, four of them Mexican
  troops. What is new on Monday 23 November: Mexican security officials
  said the United States had flown a surveillance drone over Mexican
  territory to help track Meza Flores down, after three months of
  intelligence sharing; and eight more suspected gang members were
  arrested after cartel roadblocks and arson in Sinaloa.
- Sources: Associated Press (via Military.com), 23 Nov 2026; NPR,
  23 Nov 2026; Reuters, 23 Nov 2026; US Department of Justice,
  23 Nov 2026.

  described as a reset of ties between the neighbours.
- Sources: Reuters (via BusinessWorld), 23 Nov 2026; BBC News,
